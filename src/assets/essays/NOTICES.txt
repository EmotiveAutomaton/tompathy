were supplied by Abraham Haskins. They are reproduced without pixel edits.
Generated-image attribution is recorded in provenance.json and shown by grey
borders. The owner specifically identified AI Slop, The Hatred of Inefficiency,
and And We Need Everyone's Help as generated, and Bot Farms as not generated.

the software's MIT license does not relicense these images. Exact source filenames
and copied-byte hashes are recorded in provenance.json. Older assets retained in
the source folder are not bundled unless imported by the current essay.
