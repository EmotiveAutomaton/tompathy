Try the reading

  1. Connect the engine above, then open an article.
  2. Click the ToMpathy toolbar icon, or press Alt+Shift+Y.
  3. Enable Follow pages above. New pages are captured and analyzed automatically while the panel is open; Wikipedia readings fetch up to 20 public revisions. Without Follow pages, choose Analyze manually.
  4. Choose a contributor above the reading. Open a recorded event to inspect its before/after evidence. Source passages are linked only when their text matches.
  5. Later readings can be accepted as revisions. Real preview runs are cached locally; use Load cached real preview in the reader menu for a backup. Per-person previews cannot be exported.

What you can see now

Research preview uses the installed local readers for text departures, habits, technique notes and goal guesses. Each result says what it is based on and whether it has been tested. Open a panel’s module status for timing limits or missing inputs. Recorded revisions are source evidence; personal values remain unknown without the required evidence. Preview can be turned off in Options to use the admitted path.

The engine stays available until review Chrome closes; you can close the launcher. Start Practice ToMpathy.cmd again to resume. History snapshots stay in this checkout’s ignored local folder. The articles remain live Wikipedia pages.
