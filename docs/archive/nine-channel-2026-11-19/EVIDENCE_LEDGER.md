# Evidence ledger

First edition, 19 November 2026 (Round 29). One row per result across the three programs, each keyed to the claim it bears on. Built by two helpers from the repositories' own records and receipts (Sounding Line at `005f38fa7`, the engine branch at `14e2ced`), then corrected and tallied by the theory thread. The machine copy is `EVIDENCE_LEDGER.jsonl`. No data row of any dataset was opened to build it.

**How a row is graded.** Cleared: past its registered bar with the interval clear of it, or repeated on a second sample. Leaning: the predicted direction, short of a practical bar; a result whose only bar was zero or chance is filed here. Flat: tight around zero. Against: the opposite, or a registered claim that failed with enough data. Could not say: too few people, a broken control, or a study held. Method: a calibration, a speed check, or a result in a made world.

**How it is counted.** By independent source, where a source is one kind of data. Robustness checks on the same data do not add a source. A source with results on both sides for one claim is marked mixed.

**How it grows.** Each scientific landing in a repository adds one row with a claim id. The fields are in `THEORY_HANDOFF_2026-11-19.md`, section 2.

**Whose grading this is.** The claim list and the mapping of results to claims are the thread's. The curator has not ruled on either.
