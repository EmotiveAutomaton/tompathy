## 2. What changed

- **The hub is withdrawn.** The tradables are the middle rungs of his 9 November ladder, which he now calls a marketplace. Nothing learned sits above the needs.
- **Freedom is a reading.** He calls it convergent: it shows up whatever the goal. It needs no set point and no channel.
- **FEAR keeps its name,** with his note that alarm is the default and safety is what's learned.
- **PANIC/GRIEF is the asking side and the receiving side** of the care pair.

- **CARE puts the other's set points on my bill, weighted by the kith index** (accepted). The kith index as body schema extension (accepted).

- **Round 15's questions 1, 2, 4 and 5** are agreed, and disagreement 11 is conceded by him.

### The thread's proposals, awaiting his ruling

- **"Above", corrected.** Inside the ladder, above means supplies the target. The inherited needs are the top and pass no target down; an open need keeps the ladder being rebuilt. A rung held in attention re-weights the needs and never sets their targets.
- **Three states of leftover:** bound (an answer has taken it on, even before the gap closes), traced (source known, no answer yet), untraced. Only untraced leftover feeds the next channel.
- **Map, backlog, outlook:** Knowledge faces now, Stress faces back, Freedom faces forward.
- **Two kinds of adopted value:** a currency, which buys everything, and a rule, which has no price; the split is whether the thing can be spent in pieces.
- **FEAR in three layers:** a default alarm, a triggered program, and learned safety brakes on both.
- **Neuroticism** as the trait of the two readings (how hard, how long, how dim the outlook), with the trait map drawn at the level of the Big Five's ten halves.
- **A channel is an inherited filter:** it re-weights the needs the way a value held in mind does.
- **LUST:** re-weights, turns display up, borders RAGE, and its completion changes what counts as mine. It buys Tending (the last clause is his).
- **Contempt** as a stored low reading about a person, not a channel.
- **Nest** as the casual state noun for Tending.

### How a tradable gets adopted

| Step | What happens | On record |
|---|---|---|
| Pairing | a neutral thing takes its pull from what it's exchanged for | chimpanzees worked for poker chips, and the chips' pull came from the food they bought. That's a 1937 result as a later review tells it ([Hackenberg 2009](https://doi.org/10.1901/jeab.2009.91-257); I read the review's text through a reader tool) |
| Caching | picking the goal turns into a habit of its own | a model that "[exploits the computational efficiency of habitual control to select goals](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1506367112)", which people ran without being asked to |
| Fusing | the means starts to feel like the end | "[four major antecedents of activity-goal fusion: (a) repeated pairing of the activity and the goal, (b) uniqueness of the activity-goal connection](https://doi.org/10.1037/rev0000095)", then similarity and immediacy |
| Rule-making | each choice gets treated as a precedent for every later one | "[stable preferences can be established by the perception of recurrent choices as test cases (precedents)](https://doi.org/10.1017/s0140525x05000117)" |
| Hardening | losing Belonging pushes ordinary values toward the rule end | "[The social exclusion manipulation specifically affected nonsacred values, increasing their similarities with sacred values](https://doi.org/10.3389/fpsyg.2018.02462)". 38 young men, selected in a study of support for extremist violence. It may not travel |
