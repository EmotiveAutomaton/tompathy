Notes for whoever writes the packets:

- **Test 1.** Its pieces exist as separate cards: MD1 and TR1 for ratings, MD3 and RR3 for choices. The new card puts ratings and choices in one registered comparison with common scaling. Primary: a double contrast, recent luck minus distance for ratings and distance minus recent luck for choices, each against a practical bar. Secondary: does a person's rating reactivity predict how much their choices follow recent luck? Cautions already in the record: recent luck and distance correlate at about 0.28; "Choices are not feelings"; a difference in coefficient size is not a difference in prediction; and on repeat plays the pattern depends on which reference is used.
- **Test 2.** Closeness means a rating of how close two people are (overlapping circles, or social distance) next to something one did for the other. Offers and refusals means records where a person is offered a trade against something they've said they value, and takes it, refuses, or refuses with heat. Sounding Line's own theory return says it has no closeness ratings in local sources.
- **Test 3.** Vary what the partner can see: nothing, the record of past offers and refusals, or the holder's inside. Measure the trust a refusal buys. The all-seeing arm already exists as X3.
- **A real objection to Test 1, from the level set's dissent:** a game with a running score puts the level on display. "Acts follow the level" may hold best there. A text has no score on display.

## 6. Open

**The five questions put to him** (with the thread's suggestion): the coral loop as the drawing of free energy (yes, and not a sixth need); the outlook inside Knowledge with Freedom as the stock (yes); rules as tiles priced in another currency (take it, with the who's-watching test proposed); expressions as acts with "state" held (yes); and what goes to the packet thread (section 5).

**The ten disagreements awaiting his ruling:**

1. Free energy about the present is calculable once a model is supplied. What can't be done is reading it off tissue without one.
2. The backlog can't be measured as arousal minus input.
3. Giving up money doesn't by itself bring Belonging (a stress test).
4. A count of flawless repetitions per rung can't be had.
5. Repetition alone doesn't lift a fluency to identity. A tie to something that already matters does.
6. People don't simply take the money when nobody is watching.
7. The auction study's regions are assigned by its authors to rule retrieval, not theory of mind.
8. Expressions aren't always slopes. They're acts.
9. Agreeableness isn't the trait for seen as competent. It's RAGE's threshold.
10. Births don't follow the slope. A level against an expectation is better supported.

**Also open:** the names for seen as competent, seen as kind and the outlook. Three studies that don't seem to exist and could be proposed: one that varies who sees a refusal, one that separates a slope from a level for a face, and one that subtracts input-driven arousal at rest. The workshop flagged on 9 November is dropped (corrected in Round 18): he doesn't write up or present negative results, and no decision date is set.
