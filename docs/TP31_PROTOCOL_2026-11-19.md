# TP31 change-detector protocol

This separate commissioned study uses the definition in the November 19 packet.
TP25's completed failure remains unchanged. This protocol is filed before any
TP31 teacher output or student fitting. Implementation of checks is not evidence
that a teacher or student has passed them.

The teacher is the SP1-v2 development-selected research reader, with its exact
full model ID, corrected panel, prompt/options and qualification recorded first.
The other pinned reader supplies the separate agreement comparison. No model IDs
are pooled into one target table. Missing second-reader support leaves the
agreement prerequisite unavailable; it is never invented or silently waived.

## Sentence pool and protection of acceptance items

Use only the held original releases of CoAuthor writer-typed text, ED situations,
Crowd-enVENT reports, GoEmotions comments, the EX5 Stack Exchange answers, and
Story Commonsense. Record their exact source pins and original grouping keys.
CoAuthor text must come from the verified writer-typed reconstruction, not model
suggestions or final prose with unknown authorship. No new corpus is fetched.
Source adapters and the complete intake still require implementation/verification
before an executable teacher job can be launched.

Split at whitespace following `.`, `!` or `?`, and at line breaks; retain trimmed
nonempty spans. This deterministic rule has no downloaded tokenizer and can
split abbreviations; report that limitation. Sentence identity contains original
source/document/position. Duplicate identities are errors. Preserve original
writer/conversation/story/session grouping; neighboring sentences cannot cross
an inner fitting/development boundary.

Before selection, exclude normalized exact matches to either fixed acceptance
set, including each constituent sentence of a multi-sentence protected item.
Normalization is case folding and collapsed whitespace. A matching full
document excludes all its sentences; matching individual sentences are also
excluded. Keep the 40 theme-free items and 191 revision sentences entirely out
of teacher requests. Only their exclusion hashes may enter a teacher payload.
They are evaluated once by the final student and never select a threshold.

For the two-reader agreement panel, select 2,000 sentences before answers.
Sort each source by SHA-256 of `TP31:23:sentence-ID`. Take 334 each from CoAuthor
and ED, then 333 each from Crowd-enVENT, GoEmotions, Stack Exchange and stories.
The ordering and quotas are fixed, not chosen from a teacher's success rates.
Insufficient held support blocks this panel; it does not justify new fetching
or outcome-dependent replacement. Hash the ordered selection and request text.

## Teacher definition and measurements

The fixed prompt/schema live in `tp31_teacher.py`. A passing sentence reports a
change in some person's situation that happened, is happening, or is expected.
Description, instruction, argument and definition alone do not pass. An emotion
word alone is not a change key. Output is exactly `change` (Boolean), `whose`
(`writer`, `someone_else`, `nobody`) and `when` (`already`, `ongoing`, `ahead`,
`none`). A false change must have nobody/none; a true change must identify a
person and a non-none time. Coercions, extra keys and inconsistent combinations
are invalid, kept as originally returned, and never repair-prompted.

The same fixed local generation settings as SP1 apply: temperature zero, seed 23,
thinking off, context 4096, output cap 256, three numerical threads and one telemetry
thread. Validate complete input fit before generation. Each new request kind
requires the packet's 500-text timing pilot. Its exact saved responses are reused
in later completion, never rerun as a fresh timing or scientific sample. Freeze
input shards and bounded job sizes from measured time before full execution.

Cohen's kappa uses the binary change answer on the fixed 2,000 paired sentences.
Report raw agreement, valid-pair count and kappa; a constant undefined kappa is
null. The `.60` bar is evaluated only for the complete registered panel. Invalid
responses leave incomplete coverage, not a conveniently selected passing subset.
Whose/when are retained for descriptive agreement and the later tense comparison.

Known-group checks use original whole Crowd-enVENT feeling reports and original
whole ED situations, with change rate at least `.85` in each; original EX5 Stack
Exchange answer units must have rate at most `.20`. These document checks are
distinct from the sentence agreement panel and must be reported as such. Reuse
an exact matching request only when its complete text and prompt/model/options
match. Report n, valid, positive and unknown counts. With invalid answers, give
the sharp rate range: positives/n through (positives+unknown)/n. A positive bar
requires the lower bound; the negative bar requires the upper bound. This retains
the written bars without guessing invalid labels. Report agreement with stories'
publisher affected flags separately; it is not an extra qualification threshold.

All teacher prerequisites must pass before student fitting. A source-bound raw
response receipt, exact remote exit/release, copied artifact verification and
public aggregate/ledger entry precede any target-table admission. Teacher labels
are a frozen local model's reading, never human truth.

## Student and acceptance

Use frozen MiniLM cap 128 vectors and the existing balanced logistic C1 head.
Hold out each source in turn. Within each other source, use the lowest SHA-256
tenth of original groups for inner development, with entire groups together.
Exclude normalized exact test-text overlaps from fitting and development.
Choose the lowest threshold on the fixed `.05` through `.95` grid meeting `.70`
detection and `.80` silence in every development source. If none qualifies,
retain `.50` only as a diagnostic and prohibit packing. Refit on the eligible
other-source rows and score the held-out source once. Both detection and silence
must meet their bars for every held-out source; an absent class is unavailable,
not zero or an automatic pass. Preserve all fitted heads and ordered forecasts.

The final head uses the same inner-development threshold rule before refitting
all eligible teacher-labeled fitting rows. It must be silent on at least 32 of
the original 40 theme-free items and on at least 80% of the original 191 revision
sentences. Missing/invalid teacher rows are reported by source and excluded from
supervised fitting; they never receive fabricated targets. Source coverage and
threshold failures stay explicit. Even a qualified research pack would measure
agreement with this local teacher definition, not an independently human-labeled
change detector or a maker state. Serving remains subject to the separate gates.
