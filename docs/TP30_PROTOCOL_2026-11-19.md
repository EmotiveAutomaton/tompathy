# TP30 frozen reader-share implementation protocol

Filed before TP30 target summaries, model fitting or evaluation. This implements
the November 19 card; it grants no channel, display or maker admission.

Use the exact source-specific TP28/R29 map. Embarrassment is unassigned;
annoyance, nervousness and the secondary furious/terrified aliases are not added
to the primary map. Each Go rater contributes one Boolean per channel (OR of
that channel's labels), divided by all recorded raters of that comment. Crowd's
five readers supply the corresponding proportions for its 1,200 reread reports.
The unassigned proportion is the fraction selecting any label outside the map,
including neutral. Empty and unclear responses remain in the denominator and
are reported separately. They are not forced into a channel. Read only IDs,
texts, author/rater linkage, labels and the unclear flag; no demographic fields.

Go's original raw-order comments are split by author: sort unique author keys
by SHA-256 of `23:author`, assign successive keys modulo five, reserve fold zero.
Unknown authors have individual comment keys. The published split and all raw
raters are retained as provenance, but this is a new author-disjoint split.
Exact normalized-text overlap (case-folded whitespace) with evaluation texts is
excluded from training; report counts. Cross-corpus global writer IDs do not
exist, so the across-source tests are not called globally writer-disjoint.

Two fixed training views prevent same-report reader/writer leakage:

- Go fitting comments alone predict all 6,600 Crowd writer labels and held-out
  Go reader majorities. Crowd reader labels never train this view.
- Go fitting comments plus the 1,200 Crowd reader shares predict all 24,850 ED
  writer labels. The two training sources have equal total loss weight.

Use original normalized frozen MiniLM cap-128 vectors. Reuse TP29's exact saved
vectors after source, code, model and ordered-input verification; encode only
raw Go comments absent from that completed cache. No TP29 fitting, inference or
gate is repeated. Save all new target tables, split masks, heads and forecasts
privately, with hashes. Public results contain aggregates only.

Each view fits nine independent sigmoid heads with soft-label cross entropy,
fixed C=3 equivalent L2 on weights only, and an unpenalized intercept. No class
balancing, target scaling, calibration, tuning or selection on test outcomes.
Constant training columns remain constant and are marked unsupported for AUC.
The comparison fits a ten-category softmax on the same vectors and training
rows: nine mapped categories and unassigned, selecting the largest target share
with fixed channel-order ties (unassigned last); all-zero rows are unassigned.
Unassigned is a control category, never a channel output. Both objectives use
L-BFGS, at most 2,000 iterations, and require convergence at fixed tolerances.

Test 1 reports each channel/source AUC and the equal-source mean over supported
writer-label source cells. All negatives, including unmapped labels, remain.
A source cell requires a varying training target and both test classes; missing
coverage stays null, never zero or success. Seven writer channels are primary.
Five must reach .70, including SEEKING or CARE. PLAY/LUST use strictly more than
half the held-out Go raters as a positive majority and are reported separately.
Test 4 compares profile/control on exactly the same supported writer cells;
every primary channel must lose no more than .01 AUC. No best-source selection.

For test 2, profile spread is Shannon entropy of the nine predictions normalized
by their sum, divided by log(9); a zero sum has spread zero. This normalization
defines a diagnostic only, not page shares. Disagreement is the mean pairwise
Jaccard distance between original rater label sets (two empty sets have distance
zero). Single-rater comments have undefined disagreement and are excluded only
from that correlation, with the count reported; they remain in fitting and label
tests. Intake verified that the original raw release includes such comments.
Report Spearman correlation; the bar is .20. The pair clause uses the two
largest predictions, provided both are at least .25, with channel-order ties.
Within each nominated pair's group of comments, compare the mean per-comment
fraction of raters selecting either member against all 35 other pairs. Report
all groups and all 36 comparator scores; ties fail. Every nonempty nominated
group must win strictly; no eligible comments means the clause is unmeasured
and cannot pass. This groups the card's pair claim without choosing groups after
outcomes or counting a rater twice for one pair.

Test 3 files full 9-by-9 Pearson correlation matrices of predictions and true
reader shares on held-out Go comments. A constant column has null correlations,
not fabricated zeros. No sign pattern is an additional scientific gate.

Bars use the packet's point estimates. This first rung has no added interval
qualification or refit bootstrap. Exact arrays permit later inspection without
new inference. ED remains CC-BY-NC research-only; source elicitation differs;
raters' shares and stand-in words are not measured channel firing. LUST means
wanting in general. The optional Spark rung is a separate run and is unrun.
