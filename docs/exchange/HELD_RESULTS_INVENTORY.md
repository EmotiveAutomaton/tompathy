## Release procedure

Rebuild with `python scripts/r48_reports.py` at every return. Register a newly released aggregate here before landing its card. Run `python scripts/r48_reports.py --check` before pushing. The outgoing-commit check compares every generated page and the digest to the released inputs, refuses unregistered files in the report directory, and accepts only the exact reviewed receiver release.

Sealed jobs register fine, coarse and pooled forms before analysis. Inside the seal, count distinct people in every cell and check the finest eligible form once with the installed gate. Ten or fewer people, person-keyed tables, extrema, raw text and weights stay sealed. A new populated receiver release must bind its table and native gate receipt before import. The current import accepts a header-only table. The receiver owns the three predeclared forms for TMP2 and TMP3. [Forms](https://github.com/EmotiveAutomaton/sounding-line/blob/18708a8f11e17e33bcd8717a21e330a8257bb2cd/docs/design/stage-15/week8/R48_SEALED_FORMS.md)

The retained MIDUS project notice excludes cells below ten and requires MIDUS citation and funding acknowledgment. The engine floor is eleven distinct people. CANDOR permits nonidentifying aggregate findings and credits BetterUp Labs and Reece et al. (2023), plus Cooney and Reece (2025) for NaturalTurn. PANDORA permits aggregate public findings only. The source is Gjurković et al. (2021).
