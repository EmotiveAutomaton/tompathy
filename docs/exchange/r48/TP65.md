# TP65 · How do readings differ across sentence-change timings?

**1. Question and prediction.** How do readings differ across sentence-change timings? **Registered (his, kept from 22 November).** Every output is higher on change sentences than on neither. **Registered (the thread's).** FEAR, SEEKING and LUST are highest on look-ahead sentences. SADNESS, RAGE and PRIDE are highest on landed ones. Worry is higher on look-ahead sentences than on landed ones.
