# TP63 · Do writer-level readings track self-reported traits?

**1. Question and prediction.** Do writer-level readings track self-reported traits? **Registered (his map's).** Curiosity with Openness; family, contact and acceptance with Agreeableness; status, approval, power, competition and honor with Extraversion; order, savings and serenity with Conscientiousness; worry and the unpleasant outputs with low stability; each leading its row and its column. **Registered (the thread's).** With a few short reports a writer, only worry and the unpleasant outputs against low stability clear the null heads. **Registered (print's, as the analyst reads Parks-Leduc and others 2015 and Dörendahl and others 2021).** Print agrees with the map on curiosity with Openness and on order with Conscientiousness. It parts from the map in three places: contact goes with Extraversion more than with Agreeableness; acceptance goes with low stability; power and competition go against Agreeableness.

**2. Source and size.** Class P. Crowd-enVENT, CC BY 4.0. 6,600 reports from 2,379 writers. Coverage varies by trait.
