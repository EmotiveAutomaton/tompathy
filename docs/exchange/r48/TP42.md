# TP42 · How many writers have enough texts on more than one date?

**1. Question and prediction.** How many writers have enough texts on more than one date? Fewer than 200 qualifying writers in each source.

**2. Source and size.** Class P. Stanford CoAuthor, public download. The original intake recorded internal research use and no express redistribution grant. Story Commonsense/ROCStories, public download with no dataset licence stated in the held release. The transfer source is Crowd-enVENT (CC BY 4.0). CoAuthor contributed 1,399 sessions from 61 writers. **5. Limit.** Releases overlap and writer identifiers vary by source.

**6. Withholding and release.** Story Commonsense lacks writer identities. Computing its longitudinal count requires an actual writer key.
