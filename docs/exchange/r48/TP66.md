**1. Question and prediction.** Does FEAR precede SADNESS within a loss? **Registered (his gates of 25 November).** In a text about a loss FEAR's output is highest in the first third and SADNESS's in the last. **Registered (the thread's).** It shows in recalled events and not in chat situations.
