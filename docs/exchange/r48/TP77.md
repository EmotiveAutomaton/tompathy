**4. Verdict.** **filed.** change usable, when usable, whose weak. B and C agreed on the direction of standing with kappa 0.574 [0.519, 0.627] on 1,324 jointly valid texts out of 1,500. Across 6,600 recalled events from 2,379 writers, B’s own-responsibility rating correlated 0.614 [0.598, 0.632] with the writer’s rating. The two gave the same 1–5 answer on 43.2% of events. On the saved forum prefix, B/W kappa for sentence timing was 0.523 [0.505, 0.541] on 17,124 paired sentences. ISEAR has 21,247 valid B readings and zero W readings.

**5. Limit.**

Limit: Readers can agree and both be mistaken. C read only the Q11 subset. The two rewordings use the same fixed reader and reports. CW usability requires B/W agreement. G12 stopped before the full W panel was available. The comparison measures agreement with self-reports of who caused an event. W’s saved source-order prefix covers 928 events. Its results are descriptive for that subset. The overlap follows the original forum order up to the workstation memory stop. Full-panel agreement and ISEAR reliability are unavailable.
