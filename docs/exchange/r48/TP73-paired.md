# TP73-paired · How closely do two readers agree on the same blog authors?

**1. Question and prediction.** How closely do two readers agree on the same blog authors? The paired comparison is descriptive. The registered B-only repeat prediction is recorded separately.

**2. Source and size.** Class P. Blog Authorship Corpus, dated openings with saved B and W readings. 432 complete authors and 8,640 openings. Ten openings per date half.

Pearson correlations with 95% intervals from 2,000 whole-author resamples.

**4. Verdict.** **descriptive.** Across 432 authors and 8,640 openings, B/W agreement on author means was 0.955 [0.943, 0.965] for unpleasantness and 0.382 [0.283, 0.475] for social norms.

**5. Limit.** Only 432 of 2,273 eligible authors had both readers’ scores on all twenty openings. G11 already covered 429 of them. These estimates measure agreement and repeatability for two fixed reader builds on that subset.

**6. Withholding and release.** Every aggregate estimate is available in the linked table. Per-opening scores and author identifiers remain private.
