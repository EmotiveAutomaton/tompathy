# TP73 · How do reader features behave on matched whole authors?

**1. Question and prediction.** How do reader features behave on matched whole authors? **Guess (the analyst's).** B's features repeat less than the null heads do, because a reader of content repeats less than a reader of style.

**2. Source and size.** Class P. Blog Authorship Corpus, public research release with no stated dataset licence. The matched B/W comparison covers 432 authors and 8,640 openings. The other arms use separate cohorts.

**3. Registered quantities.**

- E-TP73-TP51-20261127. Both registered curiosity contrasts miss. [All estimates, intervals, sample sizes and controls](../../../engine/validation/readers-tp73-competence-2026-11.json).

**5. Limit.**

Limit: One reader and a selected set of authors with enough openings. Matched CU controls are pending. One fixed B reader, whole blog authors with enough dated posts, and the same selected openings for every control. Only 432 of 2,273 eligible authors had both readers’ scores on all twenty openings. G11 already covered 429 of them. These estimates measure agreement and repeatability for two fixed reader builds on that subset.
