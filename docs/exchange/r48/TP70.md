# TP70 · Which groups recur among the tags?

**1. Question and prediction.** Which groups recur among the tags? **Registered (his).** The needs appear as groups: the map's grouping lies above its ROT band in both files. **Registered (the thread's, from a first look at 73 word profiles).** Three groups: standing (status, approval, competition, honor, acceptance, power), closeness (family, contact, romance) and exploring (curiosity, independence). This grouping lies above its ROT band in both files and fits better than the map's. No Meaning group: order, savings and serenity, scored as a grouping of one group against all other tags, do not lie above their own band. The body tags split. **Registered (print's, as the analyst reads the value circle).** A fourth group of order, savings and tranquility, which is the tag of that name: the four-group version fits better than the three.
