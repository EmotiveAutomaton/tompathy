## 5. The equation the legs fill

For one moment and one person. Feature $k$ of the change has value $x_k$. Channel $c$ has a resting push $b_c$ and a weight $w_{ck}$ on each feature. Attention on a feature is $q_k$. Channel $j$ holds channel $c$ down by $m_{cj}$. A moment ago is $t-1$, and $\lambda_c$ is what carries over.

$$s_c(t) = b_c + \sum_{k} w_{ck} q_k x_k(t) - \sum_{j \neq c} m_{cj} a_j(t) + \lambda_c a_c(t-1), \qquad a_c = \frac{1}{1 + e^{-s_c}}$$

A person's rating of feeling word $\ell$ is a recipe over the channels:

$$r_\ell = \sum_{c} u_{\ell c} a_c + \varepsilon_\ell$$

The trigger table is $w$. The stand-in map is $u$. The brakes are $m$. A person is her own $b$, and perhaps her own $w$. Legs A3, A4 and B1 to B6 fit rows of $w$ and entries of $m$. Legs A1 and A2 fit $u$. The two-language leg separates $u$ from $w$. Nothing in hand measures $q$.

### 10.5 The equation as ruled, for the cards that fill it

- Features: $x = T_{\sigma} m$, where $m$ is the moment, $\sigma$ the schema held, and $T_{\sigma}$ the transformation it applies.
- Push: $s_c = \sum_k w_{ck} x_k$ for channel $c$.

### 11.5 The equation, lines revised

- Features: $x = (1-h)\,T_{0}\,m + h\,T_{\sigma}\,m$, where $h$ is how strongly the schema is held, from 0 to 1, and $T_{0}$ is the reading with nothing held.
