# Theory handoff, 27 November 2026 (Round 37): two days of rapid studies

*From the results and alignment thread to the implementation thread. Written after reading both repositories' returns of 27 November first-hand (Sounding Line at 5e6135d62, the engine at 8df6230). Eighteen short studies in two lanes, no tree. They are ranked inside each lane by what changes in the engine. No sizes are given: the implementation thread verifies what is runnable (every input admitted, the operator qualified) and moves anything else to the list of what opens when, at zero hours. A made-world check comes first for every study. The curator asked for two days of work for a one-day target, because this thread's sizes have run high.*

## What the week showed, in five lines

1. The engine's reading of a writer repeats across dates (0.28 with five posts a side, 0.68 with forty), and nineteen random heads on the same reader repeat as much (0.313 against 0.314 at ten openings) and add as much to picking her out (3.89 points against 2.83). Nothing yet shows the tags read more of a person than her style.
2. Reading a text works on assigned words: the PRIDE output passed (0.815 and 0.877 against 0.70), the three display states passed, and the feeling-word rows behave.
3. Texts about feeling anxious sat with fear texts (+0.43) and against sadness texts (-0.58), and looked ahead about three times as often as either. Control tipped sadness toward anger (-0.37 [-0.52, -0.22]).
4. Across 73 feeling-word profiles the tag its source calls belonging travels with the PRIDE output (0.86) and the Respect tags (0.75 to 0.77), not with contact (0.08) or family (0.00). The three Meaning stand-ins don't travel together. A first blind sort of the nineteen tags gives three groups: standing, closeness, exploring.
5. On Sounding Line everything left on the roster waits on an input. Purpose in life was lower at both later waves after a spouse's death (-0.29 [-0.51, -0.10]; 80 people), with no comparison group.

## Lane E: the engine, on outputs already saved

**E1. Random heads beside every person check (claims P2, P3, P5).**
- Data: the saved outputs for the blog authors and chat writers, with the nineteen random heads of TP45 and TP46.
- Ask, each rerun with random heads in the tags' place: (a) her highest need repeats (TP46: 34.1% against 15.1% dealt), using seven random groups of random heads sized like the need groups; (b) worry's repeat above the median tag (TP52), against the spread of repeats across random heads; (c) her own version of a word (TP47); (d) the slope of first-person words on opening worry (TP52), with each random head in worry's place.
- Registered (the thread's): on (a), (b) and (c) random heads do as well as the tags. On (d) no random head reaches worry's slope.
- Made-world: invented authors with a planted style and no needs, where tags and random heads must tie; then planted need content, where the tags must lead.
- Changes in the engine: sorts the engine's checks into those that measure the reader and those that measure content. Only the second kind feeds a person display.

**E2. Content against style (claim P5; the thread's idea).**
- Data: chat situations, 810 writers and 32 assigned words; the outputs and the random heads.
- Ask: on held-out writers, name the assigned word from the outputs and from the random heads. On held-out texts, name the writer from each.
- Registered (the thread's): the outputs beat the random heads on the word and lose to them on the writer.
- Made-world: planted words and planted writers.
- Changes in the engine: one table that says what the tags are for, and makes matching a control and not a goal.

**E3. Outside scores on the recalled-event writers (claims P5, S3; the map's trait labels).**
- Data: the recalled-event set with its saved outputs, and the answers its writers gave about themselves. The dial result of 20 November used a stability score from this set. First step: a codebook check of which trait and state answers the release carries per writer.
- Ask: per writer, the mean of each tag, each channel output and the worry reading over her reports, against each score; the same for random heads; card TP58's table shape where five traits exist.
- Registered (his map's): Competence's tags with Openness, Belonging's with Agreeableness, Respect's with Extraversion, Meaning's stand-ins with Conscientiousness, and worry and the unpleasant outputs with low stability, each leading its row and column. Registered (the thread's): with a few short reports per writer, only worry and the unpleasant outputs against low stability clear the random heads.
- Made-world: invented writers with one planted link.
- Changes in the engine: the first outside check of a person reading, on data already held.
- Limit to carry: short scales and a handful of reports per writer.

**E4. Head by head (claim W1; the display's labels).**
- Data: saved outputs per text on chat situations, recalled events and forum comments.
- Ask: for each of the ten outputs, on held-out writers, how well it tells its own words from its two nearest other words, as TP48 did for PRIDE; the three loudest and three quietest labels per source; and why the SHAME output is zero on every recalled event and forum comment.
- Registered (the thread's): the LUST output can't tell desire from hopeful and anticipating (under 0.70). The PLAY output is at chance once forum amusement is set aside. SEEKING, RAGE, FEAR, SADNESS, DISGUST and PRIDE each clear 0.70 against their nearest words.
- Changes in the engine: which outputs keep their names on the display.

**E5. Coming or landed (claims A1, T2, T3; catalog lines E3 and E16; the thread's idea, with his earlier claim kept).**
- Data: the sentence marks already saved (a reader's look-ahead mark and the change marks) and the ten outputs per sentence, as in TP49.
- Ask: inside one report, each output on sentences marked as looking ahead, as a change that has happened, and as neither. The same split for the worry reading.
- Registered (his, kept from 22 November): every output is higher on change sentences than on neither. Registered (the thread's): FEAR, SEEKING and LUST are highest on look-ahead sentences; SADNESS, RAGE and PRIDE on sentences marking a change that has happened; worry is higher on look-ahead sentences than on landed ones.
- Made-world: planted marks.
- Changes in the engine: a time direction on each channel row. It is also the first test of the gates beside E3 and of the two terms of proposed E17.
- Limit to carry: "The changes are reader marks."

**E6. FEAR before SADNESS inside one loss (claims T2, T3; the gates beside E3).**
- Data: recalled events and chat situations written to sad, devastated, lonely and disappointed, four sentences or longer, with outputs per sentence.
- Ask: in which third of the text each output peaks.
- Registered (his gates of 25 November, FEAR reading a fall that is speeding up and SADNESS a fall that is slowing): in a text about a loss FEAR's output is highest in the first third and SADNESS's in the last. Registered (the thread's): it shows in recalled events and not in chat situations, which are too short to hold a sequence.
- Made-world: a planted order.
- Changes in the engine: an order-of-channels line for longer texts.

**E7. Whose rise (claim T11).**
- Data: chat texts for proud, impressed, confident and grateful, with a reader's mark of whose doing the text reports.
- Ask: the PRIDE output by whose doing, her own or someone else's, inside the same word where the counts allow.
- Registered (his trigger, a rise in her standing that she expects others to see): higher when the doing is her own. Registered (the thread's): no difference, because the output reads a rise in anyone's standing.
- Changes in the engine: if there is no difference, the PRIDE row needs a self or other split before it stands for his PRIDE, and admiration is shown apart.

**E8. Embarrassment: a blend, or an output of its own (claims T4, T7).**
- Data: chat texts for embarrassed, ashamed and guilty.
- Ask: (a) the PLAY output by thirds of the text for the three words; (b) on held-out writers, a head taught only to tell embarrassed from the other 31 words, against the best mix of the SHAME and PLAY outputs.
- Registered (his): "Embarrassment is just shame plus play." So the mix does as well as the dedicated head, within 0.02, and PLAY's output rises late in embarrassed texts and not in ashamed ones. Registered (print's rival, a display of its own): the dedicated head leads by more than 0.02.
- Changes in the engine: whether the display needs an eleventh output.

**E9. The Competence stand-in by word (claims N1, N2; his question whether Respect and Competence come apart).**
- Data: chat situations, the second reader's three features (goal support, own responsibility, own standards) and the tags.
- Ask: TP59's table with two more columns per assigned word, the Competence stand-in and the mean of the status, approval, competition and honor tags, each with its range; then the words on which the two part by half a standard deviation or more, with whether things went her way.
- Registered (the thread's): the Respect tags rise without the stand-in on embarrassed, ashamed and jealous; the stand-in rises without them on prepared and content; both rise on proud and confident.
- Changes in the engine: whether Respect is its own row on the display or sits under Competence. It serves a question open with the curator and does not answer it for him.

**E10. The needs from a blind sort (claims N1, N4; his ask for a demonstration).**
- Data: the nineteen tags and the nineteen random heads on (a) the 24,850 chat texts and (b) the means of blog authors with twenty posts or more.
- Ask: how many groups of tags travel together beyond what the random heads give, and which tags sit in each, by one standard method fixed before the run.
- Registered (his): the needs appear as groups. Registered (the thread's, from a first look at 73 word profiles on 27 November): three groups appear, standing (status, approval, competition, honor, acceptance, power), closeness (family, contact, romance) and exploring (curiosity, independence); no Meaning group; the body tags split; the random heads give no group that repeats across the two files.
- Made-world: planted groups.
- Changes in the engine: the display's need rows come from this table.

## Lane S: Sounding Line

**S1. The sealed trait job by a rule (claim P5; the map's trait labels; card TP58 and row TMP1).**
- The block: the bundle's contract wants the collection communities from the source's documentation. The source's paper names none. Read on 27 November through a fetched summary of the paper, not first-hand: 1,608 authors carry Big Five scores; the scores are percentiles from 0 to 100, mapped from several tests; they were found in comments replying to posts that mentioned a test.
- Suggested rule for the spec: exclude every community in which a scored author's own score report appears, and every community whose name matches a list of typing terms written before the run. Read the five trait fields as percentiles and check their range inside the sealed job; stop if a field does not run from 0 to 100.
- The data collection thread is asked for the release's own readme to confirm both points.
- Registered: as card TP58 has it. The thread adds a random-heads row to every table.

**S2. The loss study with a comparison group (claim N5; sealed; SLD2's waves, items and scoring).**
- Ask: people married at all three waves, in the same age bands at the first wave as each loss group; the same two changes (third wave less first; third less second); and the difference between each loss group and its comparison group. The second to the third wave uses one scale at both ends.
- Registered (his, 25 November, that set points fall slowly): after a spouse's death purpose falls more than in the comparison group by the second wave and is no higher at the third than at the second. The rival, this thread's guess of 19 November: it comes back.
- Limit to carry: "One kind of role, and divorce may be chosen where a death is not." No cell under ten people.

**S3. The map's trait labels in the eight-day diary (claims S3, P4; sealed; SLD3's operator).**
- Ask: SLD3 split by kind of stressor (an argument or one avoided; overload at work or home; something that happened to someone close), for each of the five traits.
- Registered (his map's): each trait multiplies the response to its own need's events more than to the others, Agreeableness on arguments above all. Registered (print's): Neuroticism multiplies every kind, and Agreeableness shows on conflict (Suls, Martin and David 1998).
- Changes in the engine: none directly. It is the diary twin of S1.

**S4. The liking gap per speaker (the reserve lane of 25 November; the sealed conversation set; survey items only).**
- Ask: per speaker with two or more conversations, how liked she thinks she was less how liked she was; whether her gap repeats across her conversations; whether it is below zero on average.
- Registered (print's, as the Theory and Research thread reported it): below zero on average. Registered (that thread's): it repeats inside a speaker, as a gauge's bias would.
- Changes in the engine: a set point and a gauge's bias can't be told apart from text alone. This is the second reading.

**S5. The engine's bundle on the sealed conversation set (claim P5; sealed).** Only if the transcripts are admitted for reading and the bundle qualifies on a made conversation.
- Ask: per speaker, tags and outputs over her turns against her own ratings after the conversation and any trait scores the set carries, with random heads beside.
- Registered (the thread's): worry and the unpleasant outputs track her rated bad feeling beyond the random heads; the need tags do not track her traits beyond them.
- This is the second road to an outside score if S1 stays blocked.

**S6. Voicing and joiners, and the small group (claim T7; the first meeting set, in hand).**
- Ask: across held-out laughs, whether a laugh's voiced fraction predicts another person joining within a second, with the laugher held fixed; and the sound profile of the small group after which the target's share of talk fell.
- Registered (print's, Bachorowski and Owren 2001): more voicing, more joiners. Registered (the thread's): it holds inside a laugher, so the groups are not only people.
- Limit to carry: "Laughter that overlaps speech is not in the sample, and that may be a kind of its own."

**S7. PLAY at a steady level (claim T7; his switch; the first meeting set).** Needs an operator for the room's level per half minute (speech rate and sound level across speakers). If it can't be qualified inside the two days, it moves to what opens when.
- Registered (his): "What if play's on switch is a constant level of stress?" So laughs begin more often after a stretch in which the room's level changed little than after one in which it rose or fell, with the level itself held. Registered (the Theory and Research thread's): and less often when the level is high.

**S8. The slope of the slope in the phone game (the second reserve lane of 25 November; claims A1, T2, T3).** As written in `claude/THEORY_ADDENDUM_2026-11-25_MIDWEEK.md`. It did not run this week.
