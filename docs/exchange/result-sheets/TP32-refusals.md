# TP32

Do writers show stable and discriminating refusal directions?

37 writers met the split-half support rule. Within-writer minus between-writer cosine was 0.047 (95% writer interval [0.016, 0.079]). Stability passed. On 1,100 sessions with different-prompt references, adding refusal direction changed top-one accuracy from 0.072 to 0.056. Gain -0.015 (95% writer interval [-0.033, 0.000]), against .030. Matching missed its bar.

Positive lower writer bound for stability; top-one gain at least .03 for matching.

Observed suggestions in public CoAuthor sessions. Person identification and channel inference are outside this study. [Aggregate and controls](../../../engine/validation/readers-refusals-2026-11.json).
