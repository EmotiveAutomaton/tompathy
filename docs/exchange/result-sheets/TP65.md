The saved sentence roster covers 24,850 chat situations and 6,050 recalled events. We will compare the ten frozen outputs inside the same report and fit the two sources separately. The primary reading uses B, with W and exact B/W agreement as checks. A sentence enters the agreement arm when the readers agree on change, whose situation it is and when. Invalid responses and ongoing changes are counted separately.

Ahead and landed each receive a comparison against neither, and ahead is compared directly with landed. The change-versus-neither comparison combines ahead and landed. A fixed effect for each report removes its mean. Within each comparison a linear term for fractional sentence position holds place in the report constant. Position runs from zero to one over the original roster. A single-sentence report supplies no within-report contrast. All 2,000 bootstrap draws resample whole writers and refit the position adjustment. A singular contrast has no estimate.

The header of the event-generation table contains **predict_event** and **predict_conseq**. We fixed those two released fields before opening their ratings for this card. We compare each with the fraction of sentences B marked ahead, among events for which all B marks are valid. The two correlations describe the timing mark. The worry arm uses the verified Q34 sentence forecasts. Further sources enter after their own sentence marks and forecasts are verified.

The original roster’s B/W agreement sets the CW status of each field. A contrast that depends on a weak mark is descriptive. A field below 0.40 kappa is excluded from dependent comparisons. Full-agreement sensitivity also depends on the “whose” field.
