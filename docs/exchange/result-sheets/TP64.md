# TP64 · Which assigned words can each output distinguish?

The fixed outputs come from TP59 and the original TP30 and PRIDE heads. The three populations are analyzed individually. The chat uses its assigned words. The event study excludes the 1,199 reports seen during the PRIDE fit. The forum roster contains 11,571 held-out comments, including 10,831 with released consensus labels. The discrimination estimates require a released label and an identifiable writer. The rescore of SHAME includes every held-out report regardless of label availability.

We use TP28’s source-specific word lists and the independent PRIDE label. The named comparisons are forum desire against optimism and excitement, and chat proud against joyful and excited. For the second method, a seeded hash assigns half of each source’s writers to select the two highest-scoring other labels. The remaining writers supply the estimates. A multilabel comment with labels on both sides of a contrast is excluded. The literal-word check excludes texts spelling their own tested label as a whole word, ignoring case. Writers who contributed an event to the PRIDE fit are excluded from its selected and named tests. Reports and known writers are counted separately.

For each comparison we report tie-adjusted AUC, the 95% interval from 2,000 whole-writer draws, its sampling standard error and its distance from the 0.70 bar. “At chance” has no specified equivalence margin. Missing PLAY gold outside forum amusement leaves that prediction unresolved. We interpret the six proposed leads as requiring both selected contrasts where a source supplies the channel’s own words. Literal-word exclusions are a sensitivity check. For loudest and quietest, we rank each label by its highest mean across the ten named outputs in TP59’s original table.

The additional SHAME reading uses the saved joint forum-and-event head. We report the score range on all held-out reports and compare shame or guilt against the other events both by held-out report and by held-out writer. The forum vocabulary supplies neither gold label. When this research head varies, that finding identifies the scope of the research head. The historical zero in the forum-only head and its provenance remain part of the result. The forum’s desire label is wanting or wishing.
