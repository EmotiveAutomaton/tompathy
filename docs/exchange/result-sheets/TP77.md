# TP77 · Agreement between readers

The original roster contains 51,557 sentences with readings from B and W. C read the 5,014-sentence Q11 subset. For each pair we count jointly valid answers, each reader’s unmatched answers and missing answers. The public tables show observed counts with B first for B/W and B/C, and W first for W/C. They report unweighted Cohen’s kappa for change, timing and whose situation changed. The timing and whose tables include the no-change category. The original two sources also receive separate tables.

The roster-wide B/W kappa sets the status used by the cards. At 0.60 or above the mark is usable. From 0.40 up to 0.60 it supports descriptions. Below 0.40, or where kappa is undefined, the studies withhold the dependent comparisons. Intervals resample whole writers within the original source identities. New-source and standing marks receive their own checks as their jobs return.

## Standing-panel readout

Q27 and G12 submit the same 43,021 texts to B and W. For each of the four fields, the registered readout reports the nominal Cohen kappa, its contingency table, single-reader and joint missingness, and a 95% interval from 2,000 whole-writer draws. Chat, recalled events and held-out forum comments each have a separate table. Source-prefixed writer keys keep unrelated people apart in the combined table. An unidentified forum writer contributes one cluster per comment.

Earning a rise has three possible answers in the submitted protocol, including “no rise.” That three-category comparison supplies the CW classification. A separate two-category table uses only texts on which both readers marked a rise. It shows how much they agree about earning the rise once they agree that it happened. The registered TP78 prediction also includes the original timing result. Its missing tail probability is calculated from the saved forecasts with the original writer resampling scheme, subject to reproducing the original contingency table and interval.
