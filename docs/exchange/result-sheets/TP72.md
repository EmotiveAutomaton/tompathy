# TP72 · Sealed reader for writing histories and conversations

The producer assembled the second CPU bundle from the verified TP58 model and tokenizer bytes. Its registered rehearsal uses invented histories, a two-person conversation passed through Sounding Line’s actual projection, and a larger invented speaker group to exercise the disclosure gate. Handover follows a successful end-to-end rehearsal and an independent byte check of the recipient copy.

The receiver supplies the excluded communities and dropped comments inside the seal. Each eligible table cell compares the trained linear readings with 200 independently drawn head sets and 200 rotated sets on the same people. The history tables record the curator and print predictions separately. Conversation ratings remain paired with the speaker being rated. Two people alone produce no releasable cell.

The history outputs are `traits.csv`, `contrast.csv`, `channels.csv`, `stability.csv`, `fixed_profile.csv`, `posts_needed.csv`, `matching.csv`, `growth.csv` and `curve_check.csv`. The conversation outputs are `conversation_affect.csv` and `conversation_traits.csv`. Definitions, input conventions and receiver checks are in the [bundle guide](../../../scripts/tmp2/README.md).

The target CPU image supplies Python, NumPy and SciPy. The bundle includes the pinned tokenizers 0.22.2 Linux AArch64 wheel and ABI3 extension. Each history, utterance, item rating, person key and person-level score stays with the receiver. The producer rehearsal uses invented data throughout.
