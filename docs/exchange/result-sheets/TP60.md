# TP60 · Proposed display contract

Filed November 24 as the last phase-two item, using the landed TP45, TP48 and TP50 results. This contract describes a research display.

**A text.** Retain the ten frozen outputs in order: SEEKING, PLAY, LUST, CARE, PANIC/GRIEF, RAGE, FEAR, DISGUST, SHAME, PRIDE. For the need profile, show curiosity under Competence; status, approval, power, competition and honor under Respect; order, savings and serenity under Meaning; and idealism under Compassion. Show family, contact and the tag named belonging individually under Belonging. The optional joint view retains the component labels for Belonging and Compassion. The body block contains food, rest, health and tranquility. Romance and independence are unplaced research labels. [Ten-output checks](TP48.md)

For the proposed TP50 state, standardize each varying output against the frozen chat reference. Take the largest standardized value separately for the tag block and the channel block. A block is high strictly above its reference median maximum. Two low blocks give “nothing at stake.” High tags and low channels give “a need in its band.” Two high blocks give “a need on the move.” High channels alone have no assigned state. Applying this to a new text requires the original calibration. The filed by-word shares describe the 32 assigned words in the reference sample. [State checks and shares](TP50.md)

**An author.** Alongside each tag level, show TP45’s measured split-half correlation, its 95% writer interval, contributing author count, split rule and number of posts per half. Select the raw or partly cleared arm to match the profile shown. The date and gap splits have separate estimates. An unmatched post count has no direct measurement. The interval describes repeatability across the study cohort.
