# Sounding Line Week 5: reviewed revision-reader evidence

**Hypothesis.** Do the changes in a revision predict the writer's stated reason for making it, beyond the frequencies of those reasons among other writers? Separately, do stable personal editing patterns add information when the post being edited is controlled?

**METHOD.** Use the frozen keyword mapping of editing comments in the April 2024 Stack Exchange archives for Writers, Philosophy, Academia, Worldbuilding and English. Predict five comment-derived reason categories from fourteen change features and six annotation-probability features trained only on IteraTeR's published training partition. Fit standardized multinomial logistic models in five editor-disjoint folds, estimating scaling and class priors from training editors only. Compare held-out macro F1 (the unweighted mean of category-specific harmonic means of precision and recall) with the exact expected confusion of stochastic training-prior predictions. Form the gain interval from 2,000 whole-editor resamples of fixed out-of-fold predictions. Independently replay all models, saved predictions, reference banks and resamples. The companion retains the frozen all-row scope, editor roles, galleries and same-post donor requirement.

**Found.** The frozen diff-feature classifier supports prediction of comment-derived stated editing-reason categories beyond the training-prior baseline on held-out editors in the five-site archive.

The table reports the complete original primary. Scores range from zero to one; gain is model score minus the prior baseline. The interval resamples editors and conditions on the fitted out-of-fold predictions. It does not include uncertainty from choosing a new training corpus or label mapping.
