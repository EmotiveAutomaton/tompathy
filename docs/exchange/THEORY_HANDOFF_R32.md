## 6. The equation as it stands, for the cards that fill it

Needs are indexed by $n$ and schemas by $s$. There are five targets: the four needs and Stress.

- Features: $x = (1-h)\,T_{0}\,m + h\,T_{s}\,m$, where $h$ is how strongly the held schema $s$ is held.
- Forecast and error: $f_n = \sum_s \beta_{ns} z_s$ and $e_n = f_n - r_n$, where $z_s$ is what schema $s$ reports, $\beta_{ns}$ is how much it counts toward the forecast of target $n$, and $r_n$ is the reading. Each schema has five weights.
- A person's weight on a target is derived: $W_n = \sum_s \beta_{ns}$.
- How much rides on a schema: $R_s = \sum_n W_n \beta_{ns}$. An identity is a schema with a high $R_s$. (The thread's formal step from his words; not ruled.)
- What is open: $\text{open}_n = (1 - p_n)\lvert e_n \rvert$.
- Stress: $S_t = \lambda S_{t-1} + \sum_n W_n\,\text{open}_{n,t}$. The thread's suggestion for Neuroticism is $\lambda$, how slowly Stress drains; his ruling of 19 November, the multiplier $\nu$ on the noise, stands until he rules again.
- The next moment: $m' = m + \nu S \xi$.
- A channel: $a_c = g_c \max(0, s_c - \theta_c)$, where $s_c$ is how well the moment fits the trigger.
- SEEKING's reading goes with $k_1\dot{c} + k_2\ddot{c}$.
- Free energy: $F = \text{surprise} + \text{reading error}$, and Stress is $F$.
- Learning, new, the thread's line built from the curator's published appreciation equation: $\Delta T_s \propto h \cdot G \cdot K \cdot \dot{c}$, where $G$ is DISGUST's gate, $K$ is trust and $\dot{c}$ is compression progress. Not ruled.
