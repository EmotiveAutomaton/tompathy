The packet is worth implementing as an evidence, coverage and observability
upgrade. It is **not an inference-speed optimization**: cheap density/timeline
metadata can reuse existing work, but sampling new hierarchy readings or fitting
new classifiers would add cost. No additional model calls, private-corpus reads,
research jobs or GPU loads were introduced by this pass.

The owner's current spoken Core values design supersedes the packet's older
“don't touch” instruction. The frozen essay and images are unchanged.

## Core values interaction

The panel is a two-dimensional scrollable table. Broader goals are above their
supporting nodes, short labels sit in dark green outlines, and supplied confidence
controls font weight. Hover or keyboard focus traces ancestors and descendants;
multiple parents are retained. Line widths use supplied weights. Unknown weights
remain neutral, not invented. The authored safe example includes explicitly
illustrative varied weights; live pages do not inherit them.

Full labels appear below the chart and in native titles. Arrow keys navigate
rows and relationships; Home/End and Ctrl+Home/End navigate their bounds. Click a
goal to hold its connections; Release or Escape clears it. Click/Enter on a value
opens the existing diagram, uncertainty and detail modal. Large floating hover
cards were removed from this table because they obscured its connections.

Short labels currently truncate the supplied phrase to four words. They are not
new inferred summaries or optimized latent vectors. Work/Author/Global remain;
real author and population values require their existing admissions. The old
zoom/background-teardrop illustration is replaced by this table.
